## ADR compliance

- **ADR-1 (passive by default):** all active techniques remain gated;
  `step` requires a recorded per-technique authorization (ADR-10), not just
  a CLI flag. OSINT is not exposed on analyze/tui/investigate.
- **ADR-9 (steps as data):** `steps/registry.py` — steps declare slug,
  label, requires, produces, answers; next-queue computed from open
  questions.
- **ADR-10 (per-technique timestamped auth):** `authorize` + `is_authorized`
  seam enforced in the `step` verb; audit entries on every step run,
  including zero-evidence results.
- **ADR-11 (checks as data):** `security/checks.py` — keyed on evidence
  requirements, never vendor. Full disclosure schema on every finding.
- **ADR-12 (assess-never-exploit):** NetAudit connects and records; it
  never authenticates, never exploits.
- **ADR-13 (consent tiers):** `use_osint_apis` is its own tier; OSINT
  consent never implies DNS consent (enforced + tested in `ip_asn.py`).
